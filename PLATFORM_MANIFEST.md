## 🎓 Complete Curriculum (18 Major Topics)

### TIER 1: Core Foundations (38,000 words)

#### 1. **Stocks Masterclass** (8,000 words)
- **Beginner:** What stocks are, ownership, dividends, why companies issue stock
- **Intermediate:** Stock exchanges, trading mechanics, fundamental analysis (P/E, PEG, Price-to-Book, ROE, FCF, free cash flow)
- **Advanced:** DCF valuation model, comparable company analysis, market microstructure, bid-ask spreads, circuit breakers
- **Real Examples:** Apple detailed analysis ($180 price, $6.05 EPS, 29.8x P/E, $110B annual FCF)

#### 2. **Options Masterclass** (6,000 words)
- **Beginner:** Calls, puts, strike prices, leverage (567% returns on small moves!)
- **Intermediate:** The Greeks (delta, gamma, theta, vega), covered calls, protective puts, call spreads, iron condors
- **Advanced:** Black-Scholes pricing model, implied vs realized volatility, straddles, butterflies, LEAPS, hedging strategies
- **Professional Use Cases:** Portfolio protection, income generation, directional bets

#### 3. **Bonds Masterclass** (4,000 words)
- **Beginner:** Bond basics, coupon, yield, inverse rate relationship
- **Intermediate:** Government vs corporate bonds, credit ratings (AAA to D), duration, yield curve
- **Advanced:** Duration mechanics, term structure, interest rate swaps, callable/putable bonds, credit derivatives

#### 4. **Technical Analysis Complete** (5,000 words)
- **Beginner:** Candlestick charts, support/resistance, moving averages (50-day, 200-day golden cross), basic patterns
- **Intermediate:** RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, Fibonacci retracement, volume analysis, breakouts, trend lines
- **Advanced:** Elliott Wave theory, Ichimoku Cloud, order flow analysis, market profile, composite signal systems (70%+ win rate)
- **Trading Signals:** Head & shoulders (95% success), breakouts (60-70%), divergences

#### 5. **Portfolio Theory Complete** (6,000 words)
- **Beginner:** Diversification, correlation, efficient frontier concept, age-based models
- **Intermediate:** Markowitz mean-variance optimization, CAPM (Capital Asset Pricing Model), Fama-French three-factor model
- **Advanced:** Multi-factor models, Black-Litterman model, risk parity, factor attribution, Kelly Criterion optimal betting
- **Real Math:** Portfolio with 0 correlation reduces volatility 30%+

#### 6. **Derivatives & Futures Complete** (4,000 words)
- **Beginner:** Forwards vs futures, leverage mechanics, margin, real commodity examples (corn, oil, gold)
- **Intermediate:** Index futures (ES, NQ, ZB), interest rate swaps, currency swaps, total return swaps
- **Advanced:** Exotic derivatives (barrier options, lookback, Asian options, swaptions, credit default swaps), systemic risk

#### 7. **Market Cycles & Macroeconomics** (5,000 words)
- **Beginner:** Business cycle phases, economic indicators (GDP, unemployment, retail sales), bull/bear markets
- **Intermediate:** Yield curve (normal, flat, inverted = recession warning), credit cycles, historical crashes (2008, 2020, 2022)
- **Advanced:** Austrian business cycle theory, debt supercycles, sector rotation, geopolitical risk, recession prediction models
- **Real Data:** 2008 S&P 500 1,576→676 (57% loss), COVID 2020 -34% in 23 days (4-month recovery)

### TIER 2: Advanced Concepts (13,000 words)

#### 8. **Behavioral Finance Complete** (4,000 words)
- **Beginner:** Loss aversion (feel losses 2x stronger), anchoring, herding, overconfidence, recency bias
- **Intermediate:** Prospect theory, mental accounting, market anomalies (momentum, mean reversion, seasonal effects)
- **Advanced:** Behavioral asset pricing models, neuroeconomics, debiasing strategies, trading psychology
- **Market Effects:** Value trap vs opportunity, growth trap, January effect (+1-2%/year)

#### 9. **Risk Management Complete** (4,000 words)
- **Beginner:** Risk vs volatility, risk tolerance vs capacity, max drawdown, position sizing
- **Intermediate:** Value at Risk (VaR), Conditional Value at Risk (CVaR), stress testing, scenario analysis, hedging
- **Advanced:** Tail risk, copula theory, expected shortfall, counterparty risk management, professional risk governance
- **Real Metrics:** 95% VaR, drawdown analysis, fat tails in financial returns

#### 10. **Quantitative Finance Complete** (5,000 words)
- **Beginner:** Normal distribution, standard deviation, correlation, covariance, z-scores
- **Intermediate:** Time series models (ARIMA, GARCH, VAR), Monte Carlo simulation, Kalman filter
- **Advanced:** Stochastic calculus, Ito's lemma, geometric Brownian motion, jump-diffusion models, Black-Scholes-Merton
- **AI/ML:** Machine learning for prediction, LSTM neural networks, reinforcement learning, NLP for sentiment
- **Real Application:** Monte Carlo: 10,000 scenarios → distribution of outcomes

### TIER 3: Professional & Specialized (49,000 words)

#### 11. **Valuation Models** (5,000 words)
- **Beginner:** Intrinsic vs market value, simple multiples (P/E, P/B, P/S)
- **Intermediate:** Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) detailed, comparable company analysis, dividend discount model, precedent transactions
- **Advanced:** Sum-of-the-parts (SOTP), residual income model, contingent claim analysis, multi-scenario valuation, Monte Carlo
- **Formula:** Stock Value = Σ(FCF / (1+WACC)^t) + Terminal Value / (1+WACC)^n

#### 12. **Trading Strategies** (5,000 words)
- **Beginner:** Buy-and-hold indexing, dollar-cost averaging, value investing, growth investing, dividend investing
- **Intermediate:** Momentum trading, mean reversion, pairs trading, sector rotation, merger arbitrage
- **Advanced:** Volatility trading, high-frequency trading, statistical arbitrage, market-neutral, machine learning strategies, risk management
- **Returns:** Value 1-2%/year premium, momentum 1-2%/year, market-neutral 5-12%/year with low correlation

#### 13. **Credit & Fixed Income** (4,000 words)
- **Beginner:** Credit ratings (AAA to D), spreads, investment grade vs junk, yield concepts
- **Intermediate:** Bond metrics (YTM, duration, convexity), credit analysis (debt/EBITDA, interest coverage, FCF)
- **Advanced:** Merton model (structural default), credit derivatives, CDOs, distressed debt, recovery rates
- **Trading:** CDS spreads widening = deteriorating credit, default probability pricing

#### 14. **Cryptocurrency & Blockchain** (3,000 words)
- **Beginner:** Bitcoin, blockchain, proof of work, wallets, Bitcoin history (2009→$69K→crashes)
- **Intermediate:** Ethereum, smart contracts, proof of stake, DeFi (lending, trading, governance), tokenomics
- **Advanced:** Layer 2 solutions, cross-chain bridges, staking economics, institutional adoption, regulatory landscape
- **Markets:** 24/7 trading, 4-year halving cycles, 10x volatility vs stocks

#### 15. **Real Estate & Alternative Investments** (3,500 words)
- **Beginner:** REITs, cap rate, real estate valuation, commodities (gold, oil, agriculture)
- **Intermediate:** Private equity real estate funds, venture capital, buyout firms, PE returns (15-20%+ IRR)
- **Advanced:** Illiquidity premium, hedge fund strategies (long/short, distressed, macro), fee structure (2 and 20), portfolio impact
- **Endowment Model:** Yale/Harvard allocate 40-50% to alternatives, 7-9%/year returns

#### 16. **International & Emerging Markets** (3,000 words)
- **Beginner:** Global markets overview, BRICS, currency risk, ADRs (American Depositary Receipts)
- **Intermediate:** Emerging market valuations, capital controls, EM bonds, BRICS details (India growth, China slowdown, Russia sanctions)
- **Advanced:** Relative valuation by country, geopolitical risk premium, carry trades, emerging market crises, FDI
- **Risk:** Currency crises (Mexico 1994, Brazil 1999, Turkey 2018, Argentina ongoing)

#### 17. **Retirement Planning & Tax Optimization** (3,500 words)
- **Beginner:** 401(k) ($23,500/year), IRAs (Traditional $7,000, Roth $7,000), Social Security (claim at 62/67/70)
- **Intermediate:** Tax-loss harvesting ($1K loss × 25% tax = $250 savings/year = $37K over 30 years), backdoor Roth conversions, HSA as retirement vehicle
- **Advanced:** Charitable remainder trusts, grantor retained annuity trusts (GRAT), 1031 exchanges, NIRP, estate planning, dynastic trusts
- **Math:** Tax-loss harvesting: 2-3%/year tax savings compounded

#### 18. **Market Microstructure** (3,000 words)
- **Beginner:** Bid-ask spreads (cost of trading), market makers (Citadel, Virtu), order types
- **Intermediate:** Order book dynamics, price discovery, algorithmic execution, liquidity/market depth
- **Advanced:** High-frequency trading (HFT), latency arbitrage, flash crashes (2010: S&P -9%), circuit breakers, dark pools, order flow
- **Professional:** Latency = edge (microsecond execution), spread capture scalping
