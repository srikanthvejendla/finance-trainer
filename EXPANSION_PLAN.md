# FinLearn Phase 2: Complete Professional-Grade Expansion

## Vision
Create a financial education platform that serves EVERYONE:
- 🧒 Complete beginner (knows nothing)
- 📚 Student learning finance
- 💼 Young professional building portfolio
- 🎓 MBA student
- 📊 Research analyst
- 👨‍💼 Senior professional/advisor

## Structure: 3-Level Approach for EVERY Topic

Each section will have:
1. **BEGINNER**: Simple explanation, real examples, core concepts
2. **INTERMEDIATE**: Deeper analysis, metrics, applications
3. **ADVANCED**: Professional-grade, mathematical, research-level
